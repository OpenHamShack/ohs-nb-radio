Made by tools/mapland-build.py from osmdata.openstreetmap.de's land polygons
(land-polygons-split-4326, data of 2026-10-28): cut to (-11.0, 49.0, 2.5, 61.0) (west, south,
east, north), simplified (Douglas-Peucker, topology kept) to 0.0002 degrees
for the fine level and 0.005 for the coarse, and cut into cells.
